# Blog goal: Longmont AI

Publish one article every other Wednesday, on the day of the meetup.

- **Published by 11:30 AM.** The article is live by 11:30 AM Mountain Time on
  each meetup Wednesday.
- **Scheduled by 10:00 AM.** The article is staged with its publish time by
  10:00 AM that same Wednesday at the latest.

Meetup Wednesdays repeat every two weeks from the anchor, Wednesday
May 27, 2026. A meetup Wednesday with no article staged by 10:00 AM, or none
live by 11:30 AM, misses the goal.

## How this file is used

The admin dashboard at admin.morahan.dev reads the block at the top of this file
and checks it against the articles in `src/articles/`. Change the goal here, not
in the dashboard.
