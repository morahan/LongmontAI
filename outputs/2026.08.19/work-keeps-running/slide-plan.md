## Image-review findings

No user-supplied screenshots. All slides use the canonical Longmont AI deck
identity from `design.md` and `public/brand/`. Source media was downloaded from
the cited pages, stripped of
metadata, and kept in the private draft tree. No personal information,
account state, browser chrome, or notification content is present. Secondary-
source TrendForce photographs were reviewed but rejected in favor of a simple
source-labeled numeric comparison, avoiding an unresolved reuse-rights dependency.
